The Tale family

Tell stories with AI that runs on your terms.

Vibewire Lab builds open-source apps for roleplay and storytelling with your own language models. Run a model on your own machine or phone, or point the apps at any OpenAI-compatible API you choose.

  • Open source, Apache-2.0
  • Local-first, no telemetry
  • No account needed

Projects

Talewell

The well every story is drawn from.

A local AI engine for Android and Linux. It runs chat, text-diffusion and image models behind one OpenAI-compatible API, with an Automatic1111-compatible image API for tools like SillyTavern.

  • Android
  • Linux
  • OpenAI-compatible API
Talewell on GitHub

Talekin

You and your characters.

One-on-one roleplay chat with your own LLM. Build characters in Character Studio, brainstorm with them while the card fills in, and keep every chat on your own device.

  • Linux
  • Windows
  • macOS
  • Android
  • Browser
Talekin on GitHub

Talefire

Stories told together around the fire.

Group roleplay for two to six players with your own LLM as the narrator. Everyone acts at once each round, joining over the local network, Tailscale or a self-hosted relay.

  • Linux
  • Windows
  • macOS
  • Android
  • Browser
Talefire on GitHub

How they fit together

Each app works on its own with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, such as LM Studio or Ollama. Together they make a fully local setup: Talewell serves the model, and Talekin and Talefire tell the stories.

  • Talewellruns the model on your phone or PC
  • Talekinfor one-on-one stories with characters
  • Talefirefor a group gathered around one narrator
